Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information Connecting Dots (the "app") and the services it uses collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or files.

Information Stored on Your Device

Your progress, credits, daily puzzle streak and settings are saved only on your device. They are not uploaded to us, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The app uses the following Google services, which collect some information automatically when you use it:

  • Google AdMob (ads) shows rewarded and interstitial ads. It may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to serve, measure and (where you allow it) personalise ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Google Firebase Analytics records how the app is used, such as levels completed, hints used, tutorial progress, rewarded ads watched and daily puzzles unlocked, together with an app instance ID, device model, operating system version, app version, approximate country, and any feature test group you are placed in. We use this only in aggregate to improve the game.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging issues a push token so the app can receive notifications, such as daily puzzle reminders and announcements.
  • Google Cloud Firestore serves the daily puzzle and app settings. The app only downloads from it; like any internet request, your IP address is visible to Google when it does.
  • Google Play In-App Review may ask you to rate the app. Ratings and reviews are handled by Google Play.

How Information Is Used and Shared

Information is used to run and improve the app, show ads that keep it free, and send notifications you have allowed. We do not sell your personal information. It is shared only with the Google services listed above, which process it on our behalf and under their own policies.

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Privacy and security in Firebase

Your Choices

  • Ad consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent before ads use your data. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Notifications: you can turn notifications off in the app or in your device's settings.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device. To ask us about data held by the services above, contact us below.

Data Retention and Security

Information collected by the services above is kept by Google for limited periods under its retention policies and is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We do not keep a separate copy.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
